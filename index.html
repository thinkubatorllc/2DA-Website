Where the name comes from

Down two.
Auto press.
All in.

In golf, when you're down two holes, the bet automatically escalates. More pressure. Higher stakes. Most people shrink. Some people find out what they're actually made of.

That moment — when the pressure auto-increases and you have to decide who you are — that's not just a golf term. That's life. 2DA exists for the people who lean in when it escalates.
