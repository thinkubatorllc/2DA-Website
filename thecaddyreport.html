How it works
Everything you need before your round.

Real-time condition reports

Golfers check in and rate greens, fairways, pace, and bunkers right from the course. Reports go live instantly so you always have the freshest picture.

✓

Verified course voice

Official course posts are clearly marked "From the Pro Shop" with a verified badge — so you always know if it's a player report or a direct update from the staff.

📅

Events & tee time alerts

Courses can post open tee times, tournaments, and specials. Players can find groups and post events. One board for everything happening at every course near you.

Built for both sides of the game
For golfers and courses alike.

For golfers

Stop guessing whether a course is worth the drive. Get honest, real-time intel from players who were just out there.

  • See live condition ratings before you book
  • Browse the events board for tournaments and deals
  • Find groups and fill out your foursome
  • Track courses you love and get notified of changes
  • Leave reports that help the whole community

For courses

Give your course a verified voice. Post updates, fill afternoon tee times, and promote your events to golfers actively looking to play.

  • Claim your verified listing with a Pro Shop badge
  • Post real-time condition updates from staff
  • Alert players to open tee times and last-minute deals
  • Promote tournaments and events to local golfers
  • Build credibility through transparent player feedback
